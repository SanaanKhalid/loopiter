---
title: Fine-tuning boundaries
---

Loopiter does **not** fine-tune or train a model automatically in this alpha. A `model`
or `dataset` candidate describes a proposed change and its evidence; it is not a
provider training job. Captured ratings are not automatically training-ready data.

To build a future application-owned training pipeline: verify consent and labels,
resolve contradictions, redact data, preserve episode/entity/time partitions, export
an approved training set and retain its hash. Use your training provider's API outside
the core. Evaluate the resulting model on a held-out audit set against a versioned
baseline, including risk-specific false-positive/negative and guardrail checks.

Then create/evaluate a model candidate and explicitly approve a deployment adapter
that can select that model version, inspect the actual serving state and restore the
old version. Model/dataset candidates cannot use autonomous deployment. Training
cost, deletion, reproducibility, drift and provider retention remain your responsibilities.

The alpha's complete starter deliberately improves a bounded prompt fragment, not
model weights. This keeps the release small and makes review, exact-match evaluation,
version selection and rollback visible. Better prompts are not guaranteed; “no
candidate passed” is a normal outcome. The architecture leaves training integrations
possible without claiming to deliver them now.
